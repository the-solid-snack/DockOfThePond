---
title: "Dinner for Three"
date: 2026-10-30
summary: "I witness the kidnapping of a helpless animal and stumble upon gold"
topics: [Travel, English]
---

On my first night in Marrakech, the guy who walked me to the riad told me something strange: "For all its lack of actual precious metal, Morocco has gold all over itself, in the heart of its people." I've just caught a glimpse of what he meant.

There are a couple of stray cats a few metres outside the riad, mommy and two kitties, living their best life off food leftovers the locals give them and overall unbothered.

Still, they don't look too big, especially the wee ones, and I made a mental note on day one to grab something for them on my way back. Little "welcome home ritual" now.

Tonight I spot the smaller one waiting for me, sitting on a scooter parked by the wall in one of those cut-throat alleys I wouldn't dare walk through in Belfast past sundown. As I make my way towards him, a local bloke materialises from behind a corner and kidnaps the lil' furball. The dude has his back to me throughout, so he doesn't see me following him at a distance.

Finally he turns into the cul-de-sac, which has exactly two doors: one to my riad, and the other to where I'm guessing he lives. It's also there that the three cats have their living space. And as I'm thinking "what's he doing now?", he crouches and drops the kitten at his feet. As he rises and turns back, he smiles at me – Salam – and leaves through the wooden door.

The mystery clears up when I finally see the scene he's left behind. Three adorable little creatures, shoulder to shoulder, noses buried deep in a fresh bowl of wet food. The guy literally went out of his way to grab the wandering one and make sure he ate too.
