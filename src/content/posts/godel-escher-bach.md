Every few years I pick this book up believing I am now a person who finishes it.
Twice I was wrong in the same place, around the dialogue with the crab, where the
cleverness stopped feeling like an invitation and started feeling like a locked door.

What changed on the third attempt was that I stopped treating the dialogues as
decoration between the chapters. They are not breaks. They are the argument, acted
out, and the chapters are the footnotes.

## Notes to expand later
