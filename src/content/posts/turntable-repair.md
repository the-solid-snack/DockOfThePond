It arrived in a cardboard box that had clearly been dropped, wrapped in a beach
towel that was not mine. The seller described it as "working, mostly" — a phrase I
now understand to mean the platter turns and nothing else is guaranteed.

Three orders and five weeks later it plays properly, and I have learned more about
drive belt tension than I expected to learn in this or any other life.
